name: reconnect
description: Identify dormant existing relationships in Ghost and draft a relevant reason to reconnect. Use for relationship upkeep and re-engaging known contacts, not cold prospect lists.
argument-hint: "[account, contact, or relationship scope]"
---

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/working-with-ghost.md`. Resolve any named account/person and establish the requested scope. Use `get_reconnect_queue` and the relationship network rather than external prospecting. Inspect actual available scope filters before interpreting "my relationships."

Assess relationship depth, last meaningful interaction, open obligations, and a supported reason to reconnect now. Respect recorded requests not to contact, explicit rejection, unresolved complaints, and evidence a contact changed roles. Inactivity alone is not a buying signal. No compelling current reason is a valid result.

For a small shortlist, verify account context and commitments. When drafting, load the user's writing style/examples and the recipient profile if available. Center the message on a real shared topic, a promised follow-through, or a relevant approved resource. Do not fabricate recent news or treat private information as public proof.

Return candidates with last interaction, why this relationship merits attention, why now, and evidence. Provide one short message per requested candidate; if no count was given, start with up to three strong candidates. Explain coverage limits instead of inflating the list.

Messages remain drafts. Do not create a campaign, launch paid enrichment for missing contact details, or contact anyone just because they appear in the queue.
