name: expansion-plan
description: Develop evidence-backed expansion hypotheses within an existing Ghost customer, tying unmet needs to product fit, stakeholders, and a validation plan. Use for cross-sell, upsell, or expansion account planning.
argument-hint: "[account] [optional product or expansion objective]"
---

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/working-with-ghost.md`. Resolve the account and read its commercial context, relevant topics, people, and commitments. Retrieve the relevant products, use cases, personas, and approved proof from Ghost rather than relying on generic product knowledge.

First establish what they already use/buy and what is unknown. Look for an evidenced unmet need, another department with a related problem, a customer-stated future initiative, or a value gap the product can address. Do not equate a topic mention with demand or infer purchase history from a product label alone.

Form a small set of expansion hypotheses. For each, provide:

- The customer's need and dated evidence.
- The offering/use case and why it fits.
- The likely stakeholder and an evidenced access path, with role assumptions labeled.
- Contrary evidence, delivery/relationship obligations, and conditions that would disqualify it.
- The next question or small validation step before pitching.

Rank by evidence strength, customer relevance, and access. Avoid invented revenue estimates. It is acceptable to recommend resolving existing commitments before pursuing expansion or to find no supported opportunity.

Use external prospecting only when the user requests a bounded search for a missing person. Present internal customer evidence separately from any shareable proposal. Do not add contacts, create opportunities, or start outreach as part of planning.
